New Scouts are welcome all year. Visit a meeting, then fill out our sign-up form.

Who can join

Youth ages 11 to 17 can join Scouts BSA. You can also join at 10 if you have earned the Arrow of Light or finished 5th grade. No Scouting experience is needed.

What a first visit looks like

  • Come to any Tuesday meeting at 7:00 PM at Journey Church. You do not need to call ahead.
  • Wear regular clothes. Nobody expects a uniform on your first night.
  • An older Scout will welcome you and bring you into a patrol for the evening.
  • Parents are welcome to stay, meet the leaders, and ask questions.

Steps to join

  1. Visit a Tuesday meeting. Every Tuesday, 7:00 PM, Journey Church, 450 Blue Ravine Road.
  2. Fill out the sign-up form. Use the Sign Up button on this page. It takes a few minutes.
  3. Register with Scouting America. A leader will help you complete the youth application.
  4. Ask about costs. Contact for current costs.
  5. Get your handbook and uniform. Both are available at the Scout Shop.
  6. Go on your first campout. It is the fastest way to make friends and start on your first rank.
